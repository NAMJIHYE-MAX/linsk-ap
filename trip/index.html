06 · What is in the price

Included, not included, and the fine print

Written so that a partner can quote a client without calling us first.

Included

  • 4 nights, twin share, in the hotel category of the tier purchased
  • 4 breakfasts, welcome dinner (D1), farewell gala dinner (D4)
  • All programme activities, classes, entrance and activity fees
  • Private coach, airport and station transfers, all local movement
  • English-speaking coordinator travelling with the group
  • Clinic coordination, booking and interpretation for medical sessions
  • Digital certificate and personal styling report per guest

Not included

  • International airfare and airport taxes
  • Visa fees and travel insurance
  • 6 self-pay meals as marked in the timetable
  • Medical treatment beyond the sessions listed in the programme
  • Single supplement, room upgrades and extra nights
  • Alcohol, personal shopping and gratuities
Medical scope

Aesthetic and wellness services only. Any medical procedure is subject to an on-site consultation, eligibility and the clinic's own consent process; LINSK does not provide medical advice.

Halal & dietary needs

Halal, vegetarian and allergy requirements are confirmed with the hotels and restaurants when the rooming list is submitted.

Changes

The order of activities may change for weather, traffic or clinic capacity. Included content is not reduced without agreement.
